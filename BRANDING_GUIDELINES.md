# M@iLi Brand Guidelines

This document synthesizes the visual, verbal, and strategic brand decisions for **M@iLi** — an AI-assisted email management solution for businesses. It serves as the single source of truth for the M@iLi brand.

---

## PART 1 — BRAND FOUNDATION

### Brand Essence
**Mission**: To eliminate inbox noise and empower businesses to focus on what matters through intelligent email management.

**Vision**: A world where email is a tool for focused productivity, not a source of constant distraction.

**Values**:
- **Clarity**: We cut through the noise.
- **Efficiency**: We save time and mental energy.
- **Security**: We protect your most sensitive data.
- **Intelligence**: We anticipate needs before they arise.

**Brand Personality**: Sharp, Professional, Trustworthy, Modern, Quiet.

**Positioning Statement**: For business professionals and teams drowning in communication, M@iLi is the premium AI email assistant that instantly triages, categorizes, and prioritizes your inbox, unlike manual filters that require constant maintenance.

---

## PART 2 — VISUAL IDENTITY

### Logo Strategy (The "Pulse M")
The primary logo is a continuous geometric 'M' resembling a digital workflow, paired with a distinct "spark" node representing the AI intelligence layer. 

**Logo Rules:**
- The `@` symbol in the wordmark "M@iLi" is a core part of the brand identity, grounding the tech-forward AI feel directly into the email space.
- **Clear Space**: Always leave clear space equal to the height of the AI "spark" node around the entire logo to ensure it never feels crowded.
- **Minimum Size**: Do not use the symbol smaller than 16px to maintain the visibility of the geometric folds.

### Color Palette
The aesthetic leans B2B premium—trustworthy enough for enterprise security, but modern enough to feel like cutting-edge AI.

**Primary Colors**:
- **Slate Black** (`#0F172A`): Represents the solid, trustworthy foundation of an enterprise tool. Used for main backgrounds and dark-mode UI.
- **Light Slate** (`#F8FAFC`): Crisp, high-contrast white used for the logo symbol and primary text in dark mode.
- **Intelligence Blue** (`#3B82F6`): The core action color. Trust, communication, and reliability. Used for primary buttons and active states.

**Secondary / Accent Colors**:
- **Neural Purple** (`#8B5CF6`): Used sparingly to indicate AI actions, insights, or automated classifications (like the AI spark in the logo).

### Typography
- **Primary Typeface**: *Outfit* (Geometric, highly legible, modern sans-serif). Used for headers and UI text.
- **Secondary Typeface**: *Geist Mono* (For data points, classification scores, and technical details).

---

## PART 3 — VERBAL IDENTITY

**Voice Essence**: Calm, competent, and decisively helpful.

**Tone**:
- **Direct**: We don't use filler words. We get straight to the point.
- **Confident**: We know our AI works. We don't use "maybe" or "perhaps".
- **Professional**: We are handling business data. We avoid slang and emojis in core product copy.

**Vocabulary**:
- **We say**: Triage, Prioritize, Intelligent, Workflow, Actionable.
- **We don't say**: Magic, Hack, Boost, Smash that inbox.

---

## PART 4 — MESSAGING

**Core Message**: Your inbox, intelligently organized.

**Value Proposition**: M@iLi uses advanced AI to instantly categorize, prioritize, and triage your business emails, so you can focus on the work that actually matters.

**Tagline**: Signal through the noise.

---

## PART 5 — BRAND DON'TS

To maintain the integrity of the M@iLi brand, avoid the following:
- ❌ Do not use literal envelope or paper airplane icons—we are about processing data, not just sending mail.
- ❌ Do not change the colors of the logo symbol to unapproved gradients.
- ❌ Do not use a lower-case 'm' or forget the '@' symbol in professional copy (Always **M@iLi**, never m@ili or Maili).
- ❌ Do not write in a passive or overly playful tone. Keep it professional.
